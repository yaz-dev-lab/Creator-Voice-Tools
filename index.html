FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before buying.

What app do these presets work with?
The presets work with real-time voice changers like Voicemod, Clownfish, or similar software. Step-by-step setup instructions are included with every purchase.
How do I receive my files?
Files are delivered through our Discord server after payment confirmation, usually instantly. You'll receive a direct message with your preset files and setup guide.
Can I upgrade my pack later?
Yes — open a support ticket in Discord and our team will help you upgrade. You only pay the difference between your current pack and the new one.
What is the refund policy?
All sales are final after files are delivered or downloaded. Digital files cannot be returned. Setup and technical support is available as part of your package. Please read before purchasing.
Do the presets work on mobile or console?
Presets run through PC software and output audio to any platform including Discord, OBS, Twitch, YouTube, and in-game voice chat. The audio output can be routed from a PC to any setup.
